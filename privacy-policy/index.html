Privacy Policy

How Rieno Network handles the information you share with us.

Draft for review. This document is a draft prepared for Rieno Network. It must be reviewed by the business owner and a qualified legal adviser, and adapted to the laws that apply, before it is relied on.

1. Who we are

Rieno Network (“we”, “us”) is an independent provider of social media account recovery assistance, appeal guidance, account management and support. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta, Instagram or Facebook. You can contact us at rienonetwork@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +92 327 8897567.

2. How the inquiry form works

The inquiry form on this website runs in your web browser. The website does not send your answers to a server and does not store them. When you finish the form, it prepares a message and opens either WhatsApp (wa.me) or your own email app with that message filled in. Nothing reaches us unless you choose to press Send in that app.

While you complete the form, your answers are held only in the open browser tab and are discarded when you close or reload it.

3. Information we receive

When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, which may include:

  • your name, email address, WhatsApp number and country;
  • the platform and service you are interested in;
  • an account username or profile link, and a description of your situation;
  • any screenshots or documents you decide to share later in the conversation;
  • the technical details that WhatsApp or your email provider attach to messages.

We never ask for passwords, two-factor or login codes, backup codes, session cookies or any other authentication secrets. Please do not send them. If you send one by mistake, tell us and we will delete the message.

4. How we use your information

  • to understand your request and reply to you;
  • to provide the services you ask for and keep a record of what was agreed;
  • to meet legal, accounting or security obligations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Third-party services

Messages you send by WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own terms and privacy policy. Emails are handled by your email provider and by Google, which provides our Gmail inbox. These services process data according to their own policies.

This website loads its fonts and assets from its own host and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. [Review note: update this section if analytics, a contact backend or cookies are added.]

6. How long we keep information

We keep inquiry messages only as long as needed to respond and to provide any agreed service, and then for a limited period for record-keeping. [Review note: set a specific retention period, for example 12 months after the last contact.] You can ask us to delete your messages at any time, subject to any legal duty to keep records.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us, or to stop contacting you, by emailing rienonetwork@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law. [Review note: confirm applicable laws and the business’s legal identity and address.]

8. Security

We take reasonable care with the information you send us. No method of communication is completely secure, so please share only what is needed to describe your situation.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults and for businesses. If you are under the minimum age required by the relevant platform, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
