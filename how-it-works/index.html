How it works

A simple process, with no surprises

From your first message to the final step, you always know what is happening, what we are doing, and what is up to the platform.

Start your inquiry

Overview

  1. 01

    Tell us what happened

    Complete the four-step inquiry and send it to us by WhatsApp or email. No passwords or codes, ever.

    About 3 minutes

  2. 02

    We review your situation

    We read your details, ask follow-up questions if needed, and explain the realistic options, including their limits.

    Clear scope and quote

  3. 03

    Guided next steps

    You decide how to proceed. We guide you through official routes or ongoing management, and you stay in control of your account.

    You stay in control

In detail

What happens at each stage

  1. 01

    You send a short inquiry

    Our inquiry form asks four sets of simple questions: the platform, the type of help, what happened, and how to reach you. At the end it prepares a message and opens WhatsApp or your email app. You press Send yourself.

    • Takes about three minutes
    • Works on any phone or computer
    • The website does not store your answers
  2. 02

    We review and reply

    We read your message carefully and reply by your chosen method. If something is unclear, we ask. Then we explain what appears to be happening and the realistic options, including their limits.

    • A plain-language assessment
    • The official options that apply
    • A clear scope and quote before paid work
  3. 03

    Guided steps, at your pace

    If you decide to go ahead, we guide you through each step: preparing information, using the official forms, reading platform responses, or setting up ongoing management. You stay in control of your account throughout.

    • You complete official forms yourself
    • We help you understand each response
    • Aftercare and security advice when relevant

Who does what

Clear roles from the start

You

  • Own the account and its login
  • Submit official forms and appeals
  • Decide whether to proceed

Rieno Network

  • Reviews your situation
  • Explains options and limits
  • Helps prepare accurate information

The platform

  • Reviews accounts and appeals
  • Makes every final decision
  • Sets its own response times

Staying safe while you get help

Account problems attract scammers. These rules protect you, whoever you work with.

  • Never share your password, login codes or backup codes with anyone.
  • Be wary of anyone promising guaranteed recovery or “inside contacts”.
  • Only enter details on official platform pages you opened yourself.
  • We will only contact you from our listed WhatsApp number and email address.
