Support · Settings and guidance

Social media support for settings, privacy and everyday questions

Not every problem is an emergency. Sometimes you need a knowledgeable person to explain a setting, tighten your privacy, or show you the right way to set something up. That is what this service is for.

Ask for support Ask on WhatsApp

When this helps

Situations we help with

  • 01

    Privacy worries

    You want to control who sees your posts, who can message you and what is public.

  • 02

    Security set-up

    You want two-factor authentication and login alerts configured properly before anything goes wrong.

  • 03

    New business profile

    You are switching to a business or creator account and want it set up correctly.

  • 04

    Confusing changes

    A platform update moved a setting and you cannot find it, or you are not sure what a feature does.

Scope

What this service includes

  • Profile guidance: name, username, bio, categories, contact buttons and links
  • Privacy settings walkthroughs for posts, stories, messages and tagging
  • Security set-up: two-factor authentication, login alerts and trusted contacts
  • Business and creator account set-up and switching guidance
  • Best practices that help you stay within platform rules

Before you start

What to prepare

  1. 1The platform and account you need help with
  2. 2A list of questions or settings you want to change
  3. 3Screenshots of anything confusing
  4. 4Access to the account on your own device during the session

Do not prepare or send passwords, login codes or backup codes. We never need them.

Process

How it works for this service

  1. Step 1

    Send your questions

    Tell us what you want to fix or understand. Screenshots help.

  2. Step 2

    We explain the options

    We reply with clear steps, or arrange a guided session where you make the changes yourself.

  3. Step 3

    You make the changes

    You stay in control. We guide you step by step on your own device.

  4. Step 4

    Check and confirm

    We confirm the settings are working as you intended and share a short note to keep.

In detail

Common support topics

A sample of the questions clients bring to us most often.

  • Two-factor authentication

    Choosing the right method and saving recovery options somewhere safe.

  • Who can see my content

    Public versus private accounts, close friends, audiences and tagging controls.

  • Message requests

    Filtering unwanted messages and limiting interactions during busy periods.

  • Business and creator tools

    Contact buttons, categories, insights and switching account types.

  • Linked accounts

    Understanding how Instagram and Facebook accounts connect, and how to unlink them.

  • Spotting scams

    Recognising fake “support” messages, phishing links and impersonation attempts.

Limitations, stated plainly

We would rather you know these now than after you have paid for anything.

  • Settings and features change often; we explain the platform as it currently works.
  • You make changes on your own device. We do not log in to your account.
  • For disabled or restricted accounts, the recovery services are the better fit.
