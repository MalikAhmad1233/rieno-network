Consultation · Start here

Account consultation: understand your options before you act

If you are not sure what has happened to your account, or which service you need, start with a consultation. You describe the problem in your own words, and we explain the realistic options, including the ones that do not involve us.

Book a consultation Ask on WhatsApp

When this helps

Situations we help with

  • 01

    Unclear problem

    Something is wrong with your account, but you cannot tell whether it is a restriction, a bug or a security issue.

  • 02

    Second opinion

    You have tried some steps already and want an experienced view on what to do next.

  • 03

    Planning ahead

    You want to understand risks to an important account before something goes wrong.

  • 04

    Choosing a service

    You are considering management or appeal help and want to know if it is right for you.

Scope

What this service includes

  • Time to explain your situation fully, by WhatsApp or email
  • An honest assessment of what seems to be happening
  • A clear list of available options, including steps you can take yourself
  • A recommendation, with a quote if one of our services fits

Before you start

What to prepare

  1. 1A short description of the problem in your own words
  2. 2Any screenshots, notices or emails you have
  3. 3What you have already tried
  4. 4What a good result would look like for you

Do not prepare or send passwords, login codes or backup codes. We never need them.

Process

How it works for this service

  1. Step 1

    Describe the problem

    Use the inquiry form to explain what is going on. It takes a few minutes.

  2. Step 2

    We review it

    We read your details and ask follow-up questions if something is unclear.

  3. Step 3

    You get your options

    We explain what you can do, what we can help with, and what is outside anyone’s control.

  4. Step 4

    You decide

    There is no pressure. You choose whether to proceed, act alone, or wait.

In detail

What you leave a consultation with

The aim is clarity. By the end, you should know where you stand and what to do next.

  1. 01

    A clear diagnosis

    What appears to have happened, explained in plain language.

  2. 02

    Your realistic options

    Official routes, self-help steps and what to avoid.

  3. 03

    An honest recommendation

    Whether a service would help, or whether you are fine on your own.

  4. 04

    A transparent quote

    If you want our help, you know the scope and cost before anything starts.

Limitations, stated plainly

We would rather you know these now than after you have paid for anything.

  • A consultation gives information and options; it is not a guarantee of any outcome.
  • We give general guidance, not legal advice.
  • If we are not the right fit, we will tell you.
