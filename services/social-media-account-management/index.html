Management · Ongoing support

Social media account management that keeps you consistent and in control

A well-kept account is easier to grow and easier to protect. We look after the day-to-day upkeep of your profiles, keep your content organised, and help you keep settings and access in order, so you can focus on your work.

Discuss account management Ask on WhatsApp

When this helps

Situations we help with

  • 01

    Busy business owners

    You know your account matters but cannot give it consistent time every week.

  • 02

    After a recovery

    You are back in your account and want it cleaned up, secured and kept in good standing.

  • 03

    Creators and personal brands

    You want a consistent profile, organised content and fewer last-minute posts.

  • 04

    Multiple accounts

    You manage several profiles or Pages and need a single, organised system.

Scope

What this service includes

  • Profile upkeep: bio, links, highlights, contact options and visual consistency
  • A simple content calendar and organised library of approved posts
  • Scheduling and publishing support agreed with you in advance
  • Settings guidance for privacy, roles, linked accounts and notifications
  • Regular account health checks for warnings, restrictions and unusual activity
  • A short monthly summary of what was done and what is planned

Before you start

What to prepare

  1. 1The accounts you want managed and your goals for each
  2. 2Brand details: logo, colours, tone of voice and any guidelines
  3. 3Content you already have, and what you are comfortable publishing
  4. 4Who in your team approves content
  5. 5A plan for safe access, such as official role or partner access where the platform offers it

Do not prepare or send passwords, login codes or backup codes. We never need them.

Process

How it works for this service

  1. Step 1

    Discovery

    We learn about your goals, audience and current accounts, and agree what management should cover.

  2. Step 2

    Set-up

    We tidy the profiles, organise existing content and agree a simple approval routine.

  3. Step 3

    Monthly rhythm

    We keep profiles current, prepare and schedule agreed content, and check account health.

  4. Step 4

    Review

    Each month we share what was done and adjust the plan with you.

In detail

Ways we can work together

Management is shaped around what you actually need. These are the most common starting points.

Profile refresh

A one-time clean-up to get your accounts in good shape.

  • Bio, links and highlights
  • Visual consistency check
  • Settings and roles review
Most common

Monthly management

Ongoing upkeep and an organised posting rhythm.

  • Content calendar
  • Scheduling support
  • Monthly health check and summary

Recovery aftercare

For accounts that have just been restored.

  • Security and access review
  • Content clean-up
  • Monitoring for new warnings

Pricing is quoted after a short review of your accounts and goals. There is no obligation to proceed.

Limitations, stated plainly

We would rather you know these now than after you have paid for anything.

  • We do not buy followers, likes or engagement, and we do not use automation that breaks platform rules.
  • Access is set up through official roles or methods you control; we do not ask for your password.
  • Growth depends on many factors outside our control, so we do not promise follower or revenue numbers.
